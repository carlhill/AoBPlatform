# AoBPlatform Project — Glossary
### 20 August 2026 · Every abbreviation used across the project. Organisations carry a link and a short profile; schemes, artefacts and technical terms are one-liners.

---

## 1. Organisations and agencies

### Health professions and accreditation

**RACGP — Royal Australian College of General Practitioners** · [racgp.org.au](https://www.racgp.org.au)
The professional college for Australian GPs (~40,000+ members) and the author of the *Standards for General Practices* — the document practices are accredited against. Two of its requirements anchor this product: C6.1A (minimum three approved patient identifiers) and the patient-feedback criterion. Its advocacy arm is also the loudest voice on AoB implementation problems.

**AGPAL — Australian General Practice Accreditation Limited** · [agpal.com.au](https://www.agpal.com.au)
The larger of the accrediting bodies that assess general practices against the RACGP Standards. Not-for-profit, operating since 1997. Accreditation through AGPAL (or a competitor) is what makes a practice eligible for the Practice Incentives Program — so its assessors' expectations effectively define how rules like C6.1A are applied in the field.

**QPA — Quality Practice Accreditation** · [qpa.health](https://www.qpa.health)
The other major general-practice accrediting agency, assessing against the same RACGP Standards. Practices choose one agency; both matter to us because either's assessors may be shown our verification evidence and feedback reports. Ask both the same question: does continuous feedback satisfy the Standard or merely contribute?

**AMA — Australian Medical Association** · [ama.com.au](https://www.ama.com.au)
The peak lobby for Australian doctors across all specialties. Called the original enduring-agreement design "unworkable" for aged care and co-drove the verbal-assignment concession. Relevant as a policy force, not a customer.

**AHPA — Allied Health Professions Australia** · [ahpa.com.au](https://ahpa.com.au)
Peak body for allied health associations (physio, OT, speech, dietetics etc.). The natural advocacy partner for extending enduring agreements beyond GPs — its members are the segment with no relief mechanism.

**NACCHO — National Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisation** · [naccho.org.au](https://www.naccho.org.au)
Peak body for the 148 ACCHOs (550+ clinics, ~410,000 patients). The gatekeeper for anything touching the ACCHO/AMS enduring pathway — including community-controlled translation governance. Do not enter that pathway without them.

### Commonwealth government

**DoHDA — Department of Health, Disability and Ageing** · [health.gov.au](https://www.health.gov.au)
Owns Medicare policy, the MBS, and the AoB reform itself — the data set, the templates, the FAQ, and the transition concessions. The policy contact is `AssignmentofBenefit@health.gov.au`. Also runs Medicare provider compliance and the Medicare Integrity Taskforce.

**Services Australia** · [servicesaustralia.gov.au](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au)
The delivery agency: processes ~480m Medicare services a year, pays providers, publishes the DB forms, runs PRODA/HPOS and the software-developer conformance regime (NOI), and will hold the enduring-agreement register. Developer Liaison is the working-level relationship to build.

**AHPRA — Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency** · [ahpra.gov.au](https://www.ahpra.gov.au)
Registers all 950,000+ health practitioners nationally. Two touchpoints: practitioner registration checks (enrolment ceremonies, deregistration hard-stops) and the National Law s 133 advertising rules that govern campaign gifts and inducements.

**ADHA — Australian Digital Health Agency** · [digitalhealth.gov.au](https://www.digitalhealth.gov.au)
Runs My Health Record, electronic prescribing, the Healthcare Identifiers conformance program (NoC/CCD) and the my health app. Not required for an AoB-only product; becomes relevant if we ever retrieve IHIs.

**TGA — Therapeutic Goods Administration** · [tga.gov.au](https://www.tga.gov.au)
Regulates medicines and their advertising. The reason pharma-funded patient messaging is a non-starter (no direct-to-consumer prescription advertising) and the source of the brand-name rules our campaign templates enforce.

**ACMA — Australian Communications and Media Authority** · [acma.gov.au](https://www.acma.gov.au)
Communications regulator; runs the SMS Sender ID Register that keeps our practice-branded messages from being labelled "Unverified", and enforces the Spam Act obligations our message templates carry.

**OAIC — Office of the Australian Information Commissioner** · [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au)
Privacy regulator: the Australian Privacy Principles, the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme (30-day assessment, no small-business carve-out for health), and guidance on the APP 1.7/1.8 automated-decision-making transparency rules commencing 10 Dec 2026.

**ANAO — Australian National Audit Office** · [anao.gov.au](https://www.anao.gov.au)
The Commonwealth auditor. Its January 2023 telehealth audit is why the AoB reform exists; its compliance audits supplied most of the integrity statistics in this project (Medicare, NDIS Commission, CCS).

**ACCC — Australian Competition and Consumer Commission** · [accc.gov.au](https://www.accc.gov.au)
Competition and consumer regulator. Enforced the $2.9m HealthEngine penalty (patient data shared with insurance brokers) — the standing cautionary tale for this product category — and cleared Westpac's HealthPoint acquisition.

**DTA — Digital Transformation Agency** · [dta.gov.au](https://www.dta.gov.au)
Runs whole-of-government digital sourcing: the BuyICT portal and the Digital Marketplace Panel 2 (DMP2) that a vendor joins to sell software to government.

**PSR — Professional Services Review** · [psr.gov.au](https://www.psr.gov.au)
The statutory scheme that investigates practitioners for inappropriate Medicare practice — 116 referrals and $27.3m in repayments in 2024-25. Its published findings (record-keeping first, care-plan misuse second) shaped the reconciliation product's exposure categories.

**AIHW — Australian Institute of Health and Welfare** · [aihw.gov.au](https://www.aihw.gov.au)
The national health statistics institute — source of the attendance, workforce and aged-care figures used throughout the business case.

**ABS — Australian Bureau of Statistics** · [abs.gov.au](https://www.abs.gov.au)
National statistics office — census language data (the multilingual tier list) and business counts.

**ASD / ACSC — Australian Signals Directorate / Australian Cyber Security Centre** · [cyber.gov.au](https://www.cyber.gov.au)
The signals-intelligence agency and its cyber arm: approved encryption algorithms, the Essential Eight maturity model, and IRAP — the security assessments Services Australia integration would require.

**AGSVA — Australian Government Security Vetting Agency** · [agsva.gov.au](https://www.agsva.gov.au)
Issues personnel security clearances (e.g. NV1) — a recommended control for privileged access under Services Australia's third-party security policy.

### NDIS and adjacent schemes

**NDIA — National Disability Insurance Agency** · [ndis.gov.au](https://www.ndis.gov.au)
Administers the $51.5bn NDIS for 782,000 participants. Its Integrity Transformation function owns the $3.7bn leakage problem, and its new claims platform (2026–2030) will check every claim against service-delivery records — the tailwind behind the NDIS product thesis.

**NDIS Commission — NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission** · [ndiscommission.gov.au](https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au)
The NDIS provider regulator: registration (including the new class 0137 for digital platforms — the "does it apply to us?" question), practice standards, and audits. Complaints grew twentyfold in five years; ANAO rated it only partly effective.

**NDS — National Disability Services** · [nds.org.au](https://www.nds.org.au)
Peak body for disability service providers — the route to the registered support/SIL organisations that are the NDIS model's money segment.

**DIA — Disability Intermediaries Australia** · [intermediaries.org.au](https://www.intermediaries.org.au)
Peak body for NDIS plan managers and support coordinators. Plan managers process invoices from the ~46,000 unregistered providers — making DIA the doorway to the aggregation channel in NDIS scenario 4.

**SIRA — State Insurance Regulatory Authority (NSW)** · [sira.nsw.gov.au](https://www.sira.nsw.gov.au)
Regulates NSW workers compensation and CTP; publishes the Certificate of Capacity — the signed consent artefact in that scheme.

**icare — Insurance & Care NSW** · [icare.nsw.gov.au](https://www.icare.nsw.gov.au)
The NSW government workers-compensation insurer (~$5bn+ scheme). Already integrated by HICAPS, Tyro and Halaxy for invoicing — the consent layer is not.

**TAC — Transport Accident Commission (Victoria)** · [tac.vic.gov.au](https://www.tac.vic.gov.au)
Victoria's no-fault motor accident scheme — $1.87bn in benefits to 43,000 injured people in FY25.

**Comcare** · [comcare.gov.au](https://www.comcare.gov.au)
The Commonwealth workers-compensation scheme and WHS regulator for federal employers.

**ACC — Accident Compensation Corporation (New Zealand)** · [acc.co.nz](https://www.acc.co.nz)
NZ's universal no-fault injury scheme (~2m claims/yr). Its scripted *verbal* patient authorisation, recorded in the clinical record, is the closest overseas precedent for consent-as-structured-data — and the reference design for any NZ expansion via Medtech.

### Payment rails and commercial

**HICAPS** · [hicaps.com.au](https://www.hicaps.com.au)
NAB-owned health claiming terminals: 30,000+ practices, 41m+ claims a year (~87% of allied-health private-health claims), plus Medicare Easyclaim, NDIS, TAC, icare and WorkSafe. Captures AoB data on-terminal; holds no retrievable consent record — which is our coexistence play.

**HealthPoint** · [healthpointclaims.com.au](https://www.healthpointclaims.com.au)
The Westpac-owned private-health claiming network (acquired 2023), resold through Tyro, ANZ Worldline and Suncorp terminals. Silent on AoB to date. (Distinct from HealthPoint Digital, the pharmacy screen network at [healthpointdigital.com.au](https://www.healthpointdigital.com.au).)

**TIS National — Translating and Interpreting Service** · [tisnational.gov.au](https://www.tisnational.gov.au)
Home Affairs' interpreting service — free to medical practitioners delivering Medicare-rebateable services, which is exactly our multilingual capture use case. Surfaced in-workflow rather than left for practices to find.

**NAATI — National Accreditation Authority for Translators and Interpreters** · [naati.com.au](https://www.naati.com.au)
The certification body for translators. "NAATI-certified" is the quality bar for every translated agreement template (REQ-LANG-03); machine translation never touches the legal artefact.

## 2. Medicare and claiming (schemes, rules, artefacts)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AoB** | Assignment of Benefit — the patient assigning their Medicare entitlement to the practitioner as full payment; what bulk billing legally is |
| **MBS** | Medicare Benefits Schedule — ~5,900 subsidised items in Categories, Groups, Subgroups and item numbers |
| **BBPIP** | Bulk Billing Practice Incentive Program — 12.5% loading from 1 Nov 2025; all-or-nothing across the practice; paid quarterly in arrears, split 50/50 practice/provider |
| **PIP / PIP QI** | Practice Incentives Program / its Quality Improvement stream — accreditation-gated payments; our campaign reports can serve as QI evidence |
| **MyMedicare** | Voluntary patient–practice registration; gates BBPIP, enduring agreements and some telehealth rules |
| **CDM / GPMP / TCA** | Chronic Disease Management / GP Management Plan / Team Care Arrangements — care-plan items; frequent PSR territory; cap the allied referrals behind rejection code 160 |
| **NIP** | National Immunisation Program — free vaccines for eligible cohorts (the "free" in campaign wording) |
| **AIR** | Australian Immunisation Register — national vaccination record; catch-up cohort source; central SMS reminders from mid-2027 |
| **NCSR** | National Cancer Screening Register — bowel/cervical register (Telstra Health, $445.75m/6yrs), SMS-first, retendered 2026 |
| **ECLIPSE / Easyclaim / Webclaim** | The claiming channels: in-hospital simplified billing / EFTPOS terminal / HPOS browser |
| **DVA · CDBS** | Veterans' Affairs and Child Dental Benefits Schedule — both excluded from the AoB requirements |
| **NHRA** | National Health Reform Agreement — funds public hospital patients; no MBS claims for them |
| **MMM** | Modified Monash Model — remoteness scale for incentive loadings (MMM1 metro → MMM7 at 190%) |
| **CE Medicare** | Chief Executive Medicare — refers practitioners to the PSR |
| **s92 · 80/20 · 30/20** | PSR negotiated agreement · prescribed-pattern rules triggering mandatory referral |
| **MPRC** | Medicare Participation Review Committee — can disqualify a practitioner after fraud findings |
| **HIA / HIR** | Health Insurance Act 1973 / Regulations 2018 — s 65C data set; 65CA/65CB enduring; 89A/89AA retention and notices |
| **ETA** | Electronic Transactions Act 1999 — s 10 e-signature test: reliably identify the assignor; indicate agreement by an action |
| **DB4/DB4E · DB020 · DB018** | Bulk bill voucher forms · Webclaim AoB form · claim adjustment form (all updated 1 Jul 2026; prior versions invalid) |
| **HW027/029/052** | Services Australia banking registration forms |
| **Basic Service Description** | Departmental MBS-to-category mapping used on pre-agreements; XML/CSV on MBS Online, quarterly updates |
| **Certificate of Capacity** | The signed workers-comp/CTP consent artefact |
| **CWA** | Complying Written Arrangement — the provider–family agreement gating the Child Care Subsidy |
| **CCS** | Child Care Subsidy — $13.6bn scheme; the closest legal analogue to AoB, different buyer |
| **CTP** | Compulsory Third Party motor accident insurance |
| **SDA / SIL** | Specialist Disability Accommodation / Supported Independent Living — the NDIS supports with mandatory service agreements |
| **GMS / STC** | Ireland's General Medical Services scheme / Special Type Consultation claims with patient signature (via the HSE, [hse.ie](https://www.hse.ie)) |
| **Support at Home** | Aged-care home program (1 Nov 2025); service agreement legally gates the subsidy claim |

## 5. Business and finance

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ARPU** | Average revenue per user — here, average annual revenue per practice (or per practitioner). The agent strategy is an ARPU play: ~$6k → $30k+ per practice |
| **ARR / ACV** | Annual recurring revenue / annual contract value |
| **CAC / LTV** | Customer acquisition cost / lifetime value — CAC is the AoB model's binding assumption |
| **EBITDA / EBIT / NPAT / D&A** | Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortisation / before interest and tax / net profit after tax / depreciation and amortisation |
| **GTM / TAM** | Go-to-market / total addressable market (the "ceiling") |
| **WC / DSO / DCO** | Working capital / debtor days / creditor days |
| **IRR** | Internal rate of return |
| **RDTI** | R&D Tax Incentive — 43.5% refundable offset under $20m turnover |
| **RCM** | Revenue Cycle Management — the US category for end-to-end medical billing administration; the overseas analogue of the admin agent |
| **PE** | Private equity — e.g. Potentia (HotDoc), PEP (Magentus) |
| **GA** | General availability — a product released to all customers, out of beta |
| **DNA** | Did Not Attend — a missed appointment; DNA rebooking is agent job #5 |
| **PLG** | Product-led growth — bottom-up adoption without a sales team (the Heidi motion) |
| **DTC / OTC / OOH** | Direct-to-consumer (prescription ads — banned in AU) / over-the-counter / out-of-home advertising |
| **MMA** | Master Media Agency — the whole-of-government campaign media buyer (via Dept of Finance) |
| **QI / RCT** | Quality improvement / randomised controlled trial |
| **FTE** | Full-time equivalent |
| **DMP2 / BuyICT / AusTender** | The government ICT procurement panel, its portal, and the tender notice site |
| **RoGS** | Report on Government Services (Productivity Commission, [pc.gov.au](https://www.pc.gov.au)) — annual service-delivery statistics |

## 6. Named programs, trials and events

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Philip Review** | 2023 Independent Review of Medicare Integrity and Compliance — $1.5–3bn leakage; pay-then-audit; 1984 assessing engine |
| **Medicare Integrity Taskforce** | DoHDA taskforce (2023–26) implementing the Philip Review |
| **FFT — Fraud Fusion Taskforce** | NDIA-led cross-agency NDIS fraud taskforce |
| **AuTOMATIC** | 2021–24 trial: practice-channel immunisation reminders beat central ones by ~6% — our advocacy evidence |
| **SMARTscreen** | RCT of GP-endorsed SMS lifting bowel-screening participation — Campaign F's template source |
| **AusVaxSafety / SmartVax** | Government-funded SMS vaccine-safety surveillance through GP/pharmacy software |
| **Robodebt** | The unlawful automated debt scheme whose legacy constrains all government ADM — detection is acceptable, automated adverse decisions are not |
