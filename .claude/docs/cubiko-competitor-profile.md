# Cubiko — competitor / partner profile

**Status:** working profile, v1.0 — 20 August 2026
**Sources:** cubiko.com.au (site, pricing, KB, blog), Pulse+IT, Medical Republic, Health Services Daily, Australian GP Alliance, Best Practice partner network, CommBank newsroom. Inline links verified by research pass; caveats in §7.

> **Headline for AoBPlatform: Cubiko is not a competitor on the wedge — it is the market's AoB *educator* and downstream *monitor*, and it explicitly points practices to their PMS vendor for consent capture.** It measures whether agreements got done (unbilled appointments, item 10997 opportunities); it does not capture them. Two strategic facts dominate: **Sonic Healthcare owns 51% of Cubiko** (and 49% of Best Practice), and Cubiko's data pipe, **Halo Connect**, is becoming the API gateway to Bp Premier. Cubiko is the model for our "beside-then-inside" motion — and a channel to court, with a Sonic-shaped caveat.

## 2. Product modules

- **Insights** (core, from **$166/mo**): 1,000+ metrics — Item Optimisation (MBS prompts), **Missed Billings incl. "Potential 10997 opportunities"**, MBS Benchmarking (national/state; After Hours, RACF, Level E), **Touchstone** opt-in benchmarking + annual report, Appointment Optimisation (DNA, utilisation, telehealth eligibility), Forecasting/Targets/break-even, Custom + practitioner ("My Cubiko") dashboards, vaccination eligibility, **PIP QI / QIM** and accreditation workflows, **MyMedicare module** (eligibility, bulk registration upload, **write-back to Bp** since Jan 2024), My CPD Outcomes (RACGP).
- **QuickCheck** — bulk real-time Medicare eligibility checks (100 patients/batch) for CDM & mental-health items. Insights add-on.
- **Calculate** (**$35/mo**) — practitioner service-fee calculation, flexible rules, multi-location, audit logging; launched with Bp May 2025.
- **Multisite** — group aggregation/benchmarking (custom pricing).
- **Cubiko Assist** — free tier (born as a COVID dashboard, Apr 2020; later free MyMedicare dashboard). A deliberate PLG funnel.
- **Satora** (dental, with Centaur) and **Cubiko for Specialists** (Zedmed-based) — their own adjacency motion, worth studying.

(Note: the feature sometimes called "Touchpoint" does not exist — the benchmarking product is **Touchstone**; the free tier is **Assist**.)

## 4. AoB posture — educator and monitor, not captor

Cubiko ran the sector's most visible AoB education campaign in 2026: a flagship explainer (31 May, updated 1 Jul 2026), a CPD-accredited webinar **with Best Practice** on electronic consent and digital-signature workflows *within Bp Premier* including "pending AoBs and follow-up management", and a **2026 AoB Resource Hub** (preparation checklist, aged-care workflow, patient/staff posters, email & SMS toolkit, historical-10997 guide).

Their stated position: PMS vendors "either already have the tools you need, or have updates releasing shortly." Their product tie-in is **downstream**: unbilled-appointment tracking, 10997 opportunity metrics, follow-up visibility. **No digital-signature or consent-capture feature exists or is announced.**

Implications for us: no product collision on the wedge; their resource hub is a ready-made map of what practice managers are anxious about; their "pending AoB follow-up" framing is exactly the completion gap our agent closes; and a Cubiko metric proving agreements are missing pairs naturally with a product that goes and gets them signed.

## 5. Commercials

Per-practice subscription scaled by size: Insights from $166/mo, Calculate $35/mo, QuickCheck as add-on, Multisite custom, Assist free. Contract terms unpublished; an independent review noted easy data removal on exit. The free-tier → paid ladder and the annual Touchstone benchmark report (category authority marketing) are both worth imitating.

## 6. Weaknesses

- **The Sonic problem** — undisclosed 51% takeover (control ~Apr 2023, practices learning of it 2024-25) drew AGPA and Health Services Daily criticism over data security and the conflict inherent in Sonic holding both Cubiko (51%) and Best Practice (49%) alongside pathology/GP interests. Independent-minded practices distrust it; Medtech-stack practices are structurally outside it.
- **Read-mostly** — with MyMedicare write-back the sole exception, Cubiko observes rather than acts. It surfaces the 10997 opportunity; a human still does the work. (Our agent thesis in one sentence.)
- Bp-centric gravity: its deepest capabilities exist only on Bp; Medtech/Magentus/allied are unserved.
- Cost stacks on top of PMS fees; recognised server/bandwidth load concern (dedicated KB page); minimal organic review footprint.

## 8. What this means for AoBPlatform

1. **Not a wedge competitor — a wedge amplifier.** Cubiko teaches the market that AoB is a problem and measures the failures; it doesn't fix them. Their 10997/unbilled metrics are the demand signal our capture-and-chase product answers.
2. **The partnership shape is obvious:** Cubiko flags the missing agreement → AoBPlatform captures and chases it → the metric goes green. An integration pitch a practice manager understands in one sentence. Caveat: Sonic's 51% means courting Cubiko is partly courting Sonic/Bp — sequence it after our Medtech beachhead is solid.
3. **Halo Connect matters more than Cubiko** for our long game: it is becoming the sanctioned API door into Bp Premier (and now Zedmed). When we expand beyond Medtech, Halo is likely our integration path — open the relationship early.
4. **Copy three plays:** the free-tier funnel (Assist), the annual benchmark report as category authority (Touchstone), and the specialist/dental adjacency motion (Satora, Cubiko for Specialists) which mirrors our consent-rail expansion.
5. **The Sonic controversy is a wedge for us too:** practices uneasy about Sonic-owned analytics reading their billing data will hear "independent, encrypted, non-repudiable, your record" clearly.
